# Vijayakumar Portfolio — 


## Publishing
Upload the contents of this folder (index.html and the assets folder) directly to your hosting repository root. Do not upload the enclosing folder as a nested directory. Keep the assets folder beside index.html.

## Contact form

The contact form submits asynchronously through FormSubmit and displays an on-page success or error message. The first time FormSubmit is used, it may send an activation email to `venkatvijay6789@gmail.com`. Open that email and approve the form before expecting visitor messages to arrive. The form cannot deliver email until that one-time activation is completed.
